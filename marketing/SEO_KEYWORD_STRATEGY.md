## 1. SEO Objectives & Search Intent Framework

AppAI operates at the intersection of **Autonomous AI Agents**, **Personal Finance/Budgeting**, and **Smart E-Commerce Automation**. Our search strategy is engineered to capture high-intent search traffic across the entire conversion funnel:

1. **Top of Funnel (Informational - "Why"):** Users feeling the pain of subscription fatigue, chore overwhelm, or curious about what agentic AI can actually do in real life.
2. **Middle of Funnel (Commercial Investigation - "How"):** Users comparing ways to automate shopping, price tracking tools, and AI shopping assistants with safety controls.
3. **Bottom of Funnel (Transactional / High-Intent - "Do"):** Users searching for autonomous procurement software, AI purchasing concierge, or alternatives to rigid merchant subscriptions.

## 4. On-Page SEO Best Practices Checklist for Blog Articles

Every article produced under this calendar must adhere to these on-page SEO guardrails:
1. **Title Tag:** Must contain the primary keyword in the first 50 characters, followed by a benefit hook and brand (under 60 chars total).
2. **Meta Description:** Must include primary keyword, secondary keyword, and a compelling searcher benefit (145–155 chars).
3. **URL Slug:** Clean, hyphenated, primary keyword only (e.g. `/blog/autonomous-purchasing-agent-guide`).
4. **Header Hierarchy:** Exactly one `H1`, logical `H2` sections matching secondary long-tail keywords, and `H3` for bulleted breakdowns.
5. **Internal Linking:** Every blog post must link to:
   - The AppAI early pilot landing page (`/pilot`).
   - At least 2 related articles in the cluster.
   - The interactive feature view (e.g., the Timetable Planner or AI Concierge overview).
6. **Schema Markup:** Article, FAQPage, and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD structured data.
