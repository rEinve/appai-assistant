## Content Publishing Cadence Summary

- **Blog**: 3x per week (Every Monday, Wednesday, Friday) — 12 articles total.
- **Facebook & Instagram**: Daily (Monday through Sunday) — 28 high-engagement posts with creative prompts.
- **YouTube**: 1x per week (Every Sunday) — 4 in-depth long-form tutorials, demos, and case studies.
- **Product Demo**: 3-minute hero walkthrough for website embeds, onboarding, and social video ads.

## Key Marketing Principles for Creators & Contributors

1. **Safety First, Always:** When promoting AppAI, always pair autonomy with its permission controls (`REVIEW_BEFORE_PURCHASE` vs `PRE_APPROVED`) and hard budget ceilings. Users fear runaway AI spending; neutralizing this fear is our greatest conversion driver.
2. **Focus on Real Chores, Not Novelty:** Emphasize practical pain points (running out of coffee, changing HVAC filters, price watches for tech gear, replenishing studio supplies) rather than abstract "AI" hype.
3. **Transparent Payment Rails:** Highlight our integration with trusted, familiar checkout partners (like PayPal) rather than opaque or unverified financial adapters.
