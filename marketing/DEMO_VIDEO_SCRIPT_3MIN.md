# AppAI Official 3-Minute Product Demo Video Script

**Target Video Runtime:** Exactly 2:55 – 3:00 Minutes (180 Seconds Maximum)  
**Total Spoken Word Count:** ~405 Words (Paced at ~138 words/minute for clear, crisp delivery)  
**Target Medium:** Homepage Hero Embed, Product Hunt Showcase, Twitter/LinkedIn Video, Pilot Onboarding  
**Tone:** Fast-paced, modern, confident, and utility-focused  

---

## Production Quick-Guide

* **Presenter / Voiceover:** Clear, warm, authoritative, tech-literate.
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (1920x1080 or 4K) with safe zones for mobile playback.
* **Audio Track:** Modern low-fi electronic beat underneath (ducked by -18dB during voiceover).
* **Sound Effects (SFX):** Subtle UI swooshes, keyboard keystroke sounds, crisp notification "pop", cash register or card confirmation chime.

---

## Production Checklist for Video Editor & Voiceover Artist

1. **Pacing Guardrail:** Ensure voiceover ends by **2:48** so the final call-to-action and end card can breathe for a full 10–12 seconds.
2. **Zoom & Pan Highlights:** During the Concierge prompt typing (0:25) and Mobile Approval tap (2:20), zoom in to 125% scale to emphasize the clean UI micro-interactions.
3. **Color Contrast:** Keep the background dark (`#0B0F17`) and ensure the status badges (`REVIEW_REQUIRED` in Amber `#F59E0B`, `APPROVED` in Emerald `#10B981`) pop crisply.
4. **Captions / Subtitles:** Render burned-in subtitles with bold yellow/white text in the lower third for 80%+ mobile social viewers who watch on mute.
