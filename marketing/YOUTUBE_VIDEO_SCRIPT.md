# YouTube Video Production Script: Introducing AppAI

**Working Title:** *I Built an AI That Does All My Shopping (Without Going Broke)*  
**Target Video Duration:** 4:30 – 5:30 Minutes  
**Target Audience:** Tech enthusiasts, productivity hackers, busy professionals, and AI early adopters.  
**Tone:** Engaging, transparent, tech-forward, and authoritative.

---

## Metadata & Packaging

### Title Options

1. **Option 1 (High CTR):** *I Let an AI Agent Buy My Stuff for 30 Days (Here’s What Happened)*
2. **Option 2 (Direct/Product):** *Meet AppAI: The First Autonomous Purchasing Assistant You Can Actually Trust*
3. *Never Manage a Shopping Cart Again. (Autonomous AI Procurement)*

### Thumbnail Concept

- **Visual:** Split screen. On the left: Person overwhelmed by 15 open browser tabs of Amazon, Target, grocery stores with red notification badges. On the right: AppAI clean dark-mode UI with a single green notification banner: *"Office coffee delivered. $32.40 under budget."*
- **Text Overlay:** *NO MORE CARTS.* (Bold yellow/white font with drop shadow).
