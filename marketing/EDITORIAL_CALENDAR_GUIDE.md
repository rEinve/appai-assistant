## Part 3: Facebook & Instagram Creative Production Guidelines

### Visual Style Guide
- **Color Palette:** Dark aesthetic (`#0B0F17` background), Slate accents (`#1E293B`), Emerald Green success accents (`#10B981`), Amber caution accents (`#F59E0B`).
- **Typography:** Modern, clean sans-serif (Inter or SF Pro Display) with high contrast for mobile legibility.
- **Aspect Ratios:**
  - Feed Posts & Carousels: 1:1 Square (1080x1080px) or 4:5 Portrait (1080x1350px - recommended for higher screen real estate).
  - Stories & Reels / Shorts: 9:16 Vertical (1080x1920px).

## Part 4: Multi-Channel Publishing & Community Distribution Checklist

Before publishing each day, execute this 5-minute distribution workflow:

1. **Social Scheduling:** Upload post copy, creative asset, and hashtags into Meta Business Suite (simultaneously scheduling Facebook Page and Instagram Account).
2. **First Comment Engagement:** Immediately reply to the post with the pilot signup link (`https://appasssistant.com/pilot`) or an open-ended conversational question to trigger algorithmic engagement.
3. **Instagram Stories:** Share the feed post to Instagram Stories with a clickable "Link" sticker pointing to the day's blog post or YouTube video.
4. **Blog Syndication (Mon/Wed/Fri):**
   - Publish on main site (`appasssistant.com/blog`).
   - Cross-post canonical URL to Medium and Substack 24 hours later.
   - Post key excerpts to Twitter/X as a mini-thread linking back to the article.
5. **Community Engagement:** On relevant days, share high-value technical discussions to Hacker News (*Show HN*), Reddit (`r/productivity`, `r/selfhosted`, `r/artificial`), and Indie Hackers.
